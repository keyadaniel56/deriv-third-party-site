    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    What is TradersUnit?

    TradersUnit is a third-party Deriv trading workspace. We provide free Deriv bots, an AI market scanner, charting, manual trading tools and analysis — all built on the official Deriv API. Trading is executed through your Deriv account.

    What is Deriv?

    Deriv is a global online broker for trading forex, synthetic indices, stocks, commodities and cryptocurrencies. It is known for unique instruments such as Volatility Indices, Boom & Crash and Step Index that are available to trade 24/7.

    How do I connect my Deriv account?

    Click “Connect with Deriv” and sign in with your Deriv credentials. You will be returned to this site with a short-lived access token. You can also paste a Deriv API token with read and trade scopes from the Deriv dashboard.

    Are the bots free to use?

    Yes. TradersUnit provides free Deriv bots and strategy templates for Volatility Indices, Boom & Crash, Step Index, Rise/Fall, Even/Odd, Over/Under and more — with no hidden charges.

    Is automated trading on Deriv risky?

    Automated trading carries the same market risks as manual trading, and no bot can guarantee profits. Our risk-management settings and strategy templates help you trade more responsibly.

    Is TradersUnit suitable for beginners?

    Yes. TradersUnit is built for beginners and experienced traders alike, with guided workflows, ready-made bot templates, charting, manual trading tools and an AI scanner that simplify analysis and decision-making.
